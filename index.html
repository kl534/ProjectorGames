Measure the lens

Hold the printed checkerboard 1½–2 ft from the camera, the whole board in view, and hold it still a moment in each place. Tilt it 30–45° a different way each time, and push it into every corner and along every edge of the picture. Red cells haven’t been reached yet.

Measure the IR rig

1. Wall markers

Each marker's centre, measured from the top-left marker's: across to the right, and down. At least four, on the wall, not in a symmetric pattern.
